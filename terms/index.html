You are responsible for the accuracy of information you enter. Depositly calculates estimates from those inputs, including its 360-day interest-year convention. Actual bank interest, tax deductions, payment timing and maturity terms may differ. Check your institution’s documents and statements before relying on an estimate.

Tracking and renewal

Depositly does not hold funds, operate bank accounts, process investment transactions or provide personalized financial or tax advice. Selecting automatic renewal changes the tracker’s projected schedule; it does not instruct a bank to renew an investment. Arrange actual renewal directly with your institution.

Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup are available only in supported versions and require your permission and a working internet connection. Restoration depends on a valid backup being available in the connected account. Device permissions, battery restrictions and other system conditions may affect notifications and backup timing. Review existing records before replacing them with a backup.
