About Depositly

Depositly is provided by Greenstone Solutions LLC as a personal recordkeeping tool on Android and the web. It organizes investments and expected payments. By using Depositly, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, stop using it.

Your records and calculations

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you enter. Calculations use your inputs, including Depositly's 360-day interest-year convention. Actual bank interest, tax deductions, payment timing, and maturity terms may differ. Check your institution's documents and statements. Demonstration records are fictional examples and do not represent your actual investments.

Tracking and renewal

Depositly does not hold funds, operate bank accounts, process investments, or provide personalized financial or tax advice. Marking a payment as received changes your tracker records only. Selecting automatic renewal changes the projected schedule; arrange actual renewal directly with your institution.

Google account, sessions, and shared backups

Optional Google sign-in requires your consent and an internet connection. It opens a personal tracker workspace; it does not create a bank account. The website uses server-side authorization and necessary session cookies to restore a valid connection when you return. Signing out ends the website session. Browser cookie restrictions or deletion, session expiry, revoked Google access, testing limits, or server configuration changes may require you to reconnect.

Use the same account on your phone and website to access the same available Depositly snapshots. Each Google account uses its own workspace and Drive data. The website and Android app exchange full backups through Google Drive; staying signed in does not make them continuously receive each other's edits.

Keep a full JSON backup and check that uploads have completed before switching devices. Review backup timestamps and existing records before restoring or merging. Simultaneous changes can create conflicting snapshots; publish one device's changes before editing the other. Loading an older snapshot may restore deleted or outdated records. Restoration depends on a valid backup in the selected account, service availability, and transfer-size limits. A JSON file is an alternative when a backup cannot fit a cloud transfer.

Local browser data and reminders

The website stores records in the current browser profile. Clearing site data, private browsing, or losing access to that browser can remove the local copy. Protect access to shared devices. Signing out of Google does not itself remove stored local records or existing backups.

Reminder preferences can be included in a backup. Delivery of Android notifications depends on the phone's permissions, alarm rules, battery restrictions, and other system conditions. The website does not replace Android's notification delivery.
