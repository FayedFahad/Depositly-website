Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

About this policy

Depositly is a personal investment tracker provided by Greenstone Solutions LLC. This policy explains the information handled by the Android app and this informational website. Feature availability depends on the app version you use.

Information you enter

Investment records may include owner names, bank or institution names, account or certificate references, principal amounts, rates, tax deductions, dates, renewal settings, payment receipts and notes. These details are used to organize your investments, calculate estimates and show payment schedules.

Local storage and manual backups

Investment records are stored on your device. You can export a full JSON backup and restore it later. CSV exports contain investment details rather than the full received-payment history. Exported files remain wherever you choose to save or share them. We do not receive your local records simply because you use the offline tracker.

Optional Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

In versions that support Google Drive backup, you may choose to connect your Google account. Google handles authentication; Depositly does not ask for or receive your Google password. The app may use your account identifier, email address and basic profile information to identify the connected account and display its status. With your permission, Depositly uploads and retrieves backup files in its own hidden Google Drive application folder using the drive.appdata permission. Those backups may include your investment records, received-payment history and supported preferences. This access is limited to Depositly’s application data folder and is used to provide backup and restore. This website does not sign you in or upload investment records.

Use and sharing of Google user data

Google account information and Drive data are used only to provide the account connection, backup and restore functions you select. They are not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI models. Depositly’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google processes information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete investments in the app. Local app data can also be removed through Android’s app settings or by uninstalling the app. Copies exported elsewhere must be deleted separately. If Google Drive backup is available and connected, local deletion and cloud deletion are separate: disconnecting or uninstalling does not necessarily delete cloud backups. You can revoke Depositly’s access in your Google Account connections, and remove stored application data through Google Drive’s Settings → Manage apps → Depositly options, where available. Restoring an older backup may bring previously deleted records back.

Website, service providers and support

This website is hosted on Vercel and has no sign-in form, advertising scripts or analytics scripts added by Depositly. The hosting provider may process request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you send. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and maintain relevant correspondence. Please avoid sending account passwords or unnecessary financial details.

Security and retention

Device records remain until you remove them. Exported and cloud backups remain until deleted or removed under the storage provider’s policies. Protect your device, Google account and exported files. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent to us, contact air.expx2@gmail.com.
