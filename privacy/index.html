DEPOSITLY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

About this policy

Depositly is a personal investment tracker provided by Greenstone Solutions LLC. This policy covers the Android app and its web companion. Features depend on the app version and the permissions you grant.

Information you enter

Your records may include owner names, institutions, account or certificate references, principal, interest rates, tax deductions, dates, renewal settings, received payments, receipt snapshots, and notes. Depositly uses these details to organize investments, calculate estimates, and display schedules. Demo records on the website are illustrative and are kept separate from your own records.

Storage on your phone and browser

The Android app stores records on your device. The website stores your records and preferences in the current browser profile using browser storage. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or losing access to that profile can remove your local copy. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to open those records. Protect the device and download a backup when you need a durable copy.

Investment records are processed in your browser. Depositly does not operate an application server that receives those records simply because you use the local tracker. Records are transmitted to Google when you choose cloud backup or restoration.

Google account connection

You may connect your Google account to access Depositly backups. Google handles account selection, authentication, and permission consent; Depositly does not ask for or receive your Google password. Basic account information, such as your identifier, display name, email address, and profile image, identifies the account you connect. Google's identity library and account services communicate with Google when used.

The website requests short-lived access tokens for Google APIs and keeps them in page memory, rather than including them in exports or saving them to browser storage. Reloading or token expiry can require reconnecting. Android authorization tokens are managed by Google's native authorization services and are not included in investment backups.

Private Google Drive backups

With your permission, Depositly reads and creates full backup snapshots in its hidden Google Drive application-data folder using the drive.appdata permission. This access does not provide access to your other Drive documents. The web and Android OAuth clients must belong to the same Google Cloud project to use the same application-data folder.

Backups may include all Self and Others investments, receipt history and snapshots, currency, number format, theme, default deposit view, and reminder preferences. They do not include your Google password or access tokens. The website lets you load, merge, or publish snapshots explicitly and can check for newer snapshots while open. A snapshot backup is not continuous live synchronization: review the account and backup date before replacing local records.

Website uploads create new snapshots without deleting earlier cloud files. The current Android app retains the ten newest snapshots after a successful upload. Copies may also be removed by you or under Google's storage and account policies.

Manual exports

You can download a complete JSON backup and import it later without signing in. CSV contains investment details and does not preserve the complete payment history. Downloaded files remain wherever you save or share them and may contain personal financial information. Delete exported copies separately when you no longer need them.

Use and sharing of Google user data

Google account information and Drive data are used to provide the account connection, backup, and restoration functions you select. They are not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Depositly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google processes information under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete investments in Depositly. Clear this website's storage in your browser settings to remove its local records; use Android app settings or uninstall the app to remove its device copy. Signing out ends the account connection but does not itself erase local records, exported files, or cloud backups. Restoring an older backup can bring previously deleted records back.

Revoke Depositly's Google authorization in your Google Account connections. You can remove application data through Google Drive's Settings → Manage apps → Depositly options, where available. Local deletion and cloud deletion are separate actions.

Website hosting and support

The website is designed for hosting on Vercel. The hosting provider may process request information, including IP address, browser information, and access logs, to deliver and protect the site. Depositly adds no advertising or analytics scripts. Connecting Google uses Google's identity and API services.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the details you send. Support messages are kept as needed to respond and maintain relevant correspondence. Avoid sending passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary financial details.

Security and retention

Local records remain until you remove them or the device or browser clears them. Exported files remain where you save them. Protect your device, Google account, and backups. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change. For privacy questions about information you have sent to us, contact air.expx2@gmail.com.
