Install or update

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone and open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  3. Confirm Install or Update in Android’s installer.

Install over your existing Depositly app to keep your records. Keep the app installed during the update.

After installing a version with app updates, open Settings → App updates to check, download and install future releases.
